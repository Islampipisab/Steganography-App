Encode text to barcode

Enter plain text (or use Add Image), then click Generate barcode. You will get a barcode PNG that can be decoded directly, or hidden later inside a cover image.

Decode barcode image

    
  

  

    Upload the original barcode PNG generated from Encode, then click Decode barcode to recover the embedded text or image payload.
  

  
Use the barcode PNG from Encode (or a saved barcode file). Do not upload a stego/cover image here.

Hide text in cover image

    
  

  

    Type the secret text, upload a cover image, choose a method (LSB/DCT/Hybrid), then click Hide text. The app downloads a stego image containing your text.
  

  
  
  
  

    
  

  
Runtime: —

  

  

    

    

  






  

    
Hide barcode in cover image

    
  

  

    Upload a barcode image and a cover image, choose a method, then click Hide barcode. Keep note of the selected method because extraction must use the same one.
  

  
  
  
  
On Extract, choose the same method you used here. LSB, DCT, and Hybrid embed in different ways—mixing them produces garbage or errors.

Extract from stego image

    
  

  

    Upload a stego image, choose Text or Barcode (decode), and select the same method used during hiding. Click Extract to recover the data.
  

  
  
  
  
Must match Hide Barcode → Method (or Hide Text). If you used DCT with JPEG-robust, keep the stego as PNG from the site when possible—re-saving as JPEG in Photos can break normal DCT unless you hid with JPEG-robust.
